Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the planner and start. Your plan is saved in your browser on your device.

How do I get Pro?

Buy it on Gumroad. You will get a licence key. Open the planner, tap Settings, then Unlock Pro, paste the key and tap Activate.

Can I use it on my phone and my computer?

Yes. Your plan is saved on each device separately. Use Backup on one device and open the file on the other to move it. Your Pro key works on all your own devices.

What happens if I clear my browser data?

Your saved plan goes with it, so use Backup now and then. Keep your licence key email so you can enter it again.

Is my information sent anywhere?

No. Your children's names and timetable stay in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Gumroad. If Pro does not work for you, contact us through your Gumroad receipt.
